Questions

Quick answers.

The basics before you choose a plan.

What do I actually get?

A saved workspace for clients, leads, jobs, invoices and expenses, plus editable quotes, checklists and customer messages. Choose a business type for relevant starting templates. You can also use 13 calculators and drafting tools.

Can I customise everything?

Yes. The system is designed to be edited around your services, pricing, customers and way of working.

What can I do on the Free plan?

Use one business, 2 clients, 2 leads, 2 jobs, 2 invoices and 2 expenses. Quotes, checklists, messages and planning tools are included. Each record type has its own limit.

Do my limits reset each month?

No. Limits apply to records currently saved in each business, including completed jobs and paid invoices. Editing a record does not use another slot. Existing records stay accessible if you downgrade.

Will this send invoices or collect payments?

You can record and print invoices, and copy or download messages. Sending, payment collection and automatic reminders are not included.
