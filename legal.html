Privacy notice

BizStacks helps account holders organise businesses, clients, leads, jobs, invoices, expenses and editable documents. This notice describes the information used to provide those features.

What information is used

When you register, you provide your name, email address, password, confirmation that you are 18 or older and acceptance of the Terms. Authentication is handled by Supabase. The application does not put your password in business records. We record the accepted policy version and the server time of registration. The age confirmation is a declaration; it is not identity verification.

You choose what business and customer information to save: names, contact details, addresses, appointment dates, amounts, notes, quotes and messages. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, medical information or other unnecessary sensitive information in these fields. The service is intended for adult business users.

Purposes and choices

Account details are used to provide access, authenticate users, respond to requests and protect the service. Business records are processed to store, display and export the work you request. Required registration fields are needed to open an account; optional business fields may be left blank. Public calculators run in the browser and do not automatically save their results to your account.

Marketing is off by default. The account's email-preference control records an opt-out. Account confirmation, password recovery and necessary operational messages are separate. No marketing sender is connected in this release. Before one is introduced, the operator must connect saved preferences and provide an unsubscribe link in every marketing message.

Service providers and international processing

Supabase provides authentication and database services. Vercel hosts the website. The operator's configured email service delivers authentication emails. If bot protection is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser signals for abuse prevention. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect their services.

Authorised operator access is limited to support, security and administering the service. Customer records should only be entered by someone authorised to use them. A business using BizStacks remains responsible for its own customer notices and lawful handling of customer information. Any required data-processing agreement must be completed with the operator.

Access, correction, deletion and retention

You can view and edit dashboard records, export individual record types and download current dashboard data from Account & privacy. Contact the operator for legacy records or other information not included in the export, to object to processing, or to request correction or deletion.

The Delete account control requires a fresh password sign-in and explicit confirmation. It deletes the account and associated business records from the live database. Active paid billing must first be resolved to avoid leaving recurring charges running. A database error or an unresolved file-cleanup requirement stops deletion and allows you to contact support. External provider records and backup copies are not erased by the live-database action.

Terms of use

These draft terms describe the current BizStacks service. The operator's identity and contact details appear below. Use an account only if you are 18 or older and authorised to manage the information you enter. Keep your login details private and notify the operator if you suspect misuse.

BizStacks provides record tracking, editable templates and planning calculators. You remain responsible for checking amounts, tax treatment, wording, customer permissions and the suitability of generated documents. Templates and calculations are general tools, not professional legal, tax or financial advice. No income, traffic, business success or regulatory outcome is guaranteed.

The Free plan includes one business and two saved records of each type: clients, leads, jobs, invoices and expenses. Business is advertised at R297/month with up to three businesses and ten records of each type per business. Business Pro is advertised at R597/month with unlimited businesses and those record types. The existing annual prices are shown on the pricing page. These are separate limits on saved records, not monthly allowances. Existing records remain accessible after a downgrade. Quotes, checklists and messages have no plan-based limit.

Paid upgrades are not open in this release. No charge is created by choosing a paid plan here. Before payments open, checkout must clearly state the total price, applicable taxes, billing period, renewal terms and how to cancel; a payment provider and verified subscription handling must be connected.

You retain rights to the information you supply. You allow the processing needed to provide the service. Do not upload unlawful material, copy material you have no right to use, interfere with other accounts, bypass access controls or misuse the service. General templates may also be available to other users.

Service availability can be affected by maintenance or provider outages. Keep your own copies of important records. Nothing in these terms excludes consumer rights or liability that applicable law does not permit to be excluded. Material changes to paid terms must be communicated before they take effect and before any required agreement is requested.

Cancellation and refunds

The Free plan has no subscription charge. Account & privacy includes a Cancel subscription control that checks the account's saved subscription status.

If an existing paid subscription is recorded, this release can save a cancellation request for the operator. It does not yet contact a payment provider or confirm that recurring charges have stopped. Until the billing integration is complete, contact the operator or use the payment provider's cancellation control and obtain confirmation. The operator must monitor and process pending requests.

Account deletion and billing cancellation are distinct actions. Before deleting an account with paid billing, confirm that renewal has stopped. Refund requests, duplicate charges, unauthorised payments and any statutory cancellation rights must be reviewed by the operator under the applicable law and purchase terms. There is no blanket waiver of statutory rights.

Cookies and browser storage

The current application does not load advertising pixels or analytics trackers. Necessary authentication tokens are stored in browser local storage by Supabase. BizStacks stores your selected business ID to reopen that workspace. Logging out ends the local login; use a private device and log out on shared computers.

This is browser token storage, not an HttpOnly cookie session. If optional analytics or advertising is added later, it must be disclosed and held back until any required consent is obtained. A decorative cookie banner does not control third-party tracking. Bot protection, when enabled, is used for authentication security.
